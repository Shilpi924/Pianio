## Full Description

Pianio makes learning piano interactive, intelligent, and highly accessible across all age groups. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to improve your skills, Pianio provides a premium interactive experience right on your device.

### 🎹 Why Choose Pianio?

**Gamified Learning**
- Structured, level-based curriculum that tracks your progress
- Earn experience points (XP) as you master new skills
- Unlock harder songs as you advance through levels
- Achievements system to celebrate your milestones

**Interactive Features**
- Real-time visual hand guides that show you exactly which fingers to use
- Falling notes display for intuitive learning
- Professional sheet music rendering powered by VexFlow
- Adaptive "Smart Tutor" that adjusts tempo when you struggle

**Multiple Input Methods**
- Connect your MIDI keyboard via USB for the best experience
- Use your device microphone to play on a real acoustic piano
- Touch screen support for on-the-go practice

**Comprehensive Curriculum**
- Beginner to advanced lessons
- Note naming and sight reading exercises
- Chord and scale training
- Ear training and interval recognition
- Rhythm training exercises
- Hand positioning guidance

**Creative Features**
- Free play mode with various instrument sounds
- DJ remix studio with drum beats and backing tracks
- Record your own multi-track masterpieces
- Import songs from MusicBrainz database
- Upload custom Public Domain scores

**Family Friendly**
- Multi-profile support for the whole family
- Progress tracking for each user
- Google Sign-In for cloud backup
- Safe and educational content for all ages

### 🎯 Perfect For
- Kids and teens learning piano
- Adults who want to pick up a new hobby
- Music students supplementing their lessons
- Anyone who wants to learn piano at their own pace

### 🌟 Key Features
- No prior musical experience required
- Works offline for built-in lessons
- Beautiful, intuitive interface
- Regular content updates
- Available on web, Android, and desktop

Download Pianio today and start your musical journey!
