# Pianio Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## Introduction

Pianio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Pianio mobile application and related services.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide
- **Profile Information**: When you create a profile, we may collect your name, age (for age-appropriate content), and preferred learning level.
- **Google Account Information**: If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and basic profile information for authentication purposes.

### 2. Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you use the app, including lessons completed, practice time, achievements earned, and progress statistics.
- **Device Information**: We collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and app improvement purposes.
- **Performance Data**: We collect data about app performance, crash reports, and technical issues to improve the app's stability.

### 3. Audio and MIDI Data
- **Microphone Access**: With your permission, we may access your device microphone to detect notes played on an acoustic piano for pitch detection purposes. This audio data is processed locally on your device and is not stored or transmitted.
- **MIDI Data**: When you connect a MIDI keyboard, we receive MIDI note data for real-time note detection. This data is processed locally and not stored.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Provide and improve our piano learning services
- Track your learning progress and personalize your experience
- Enable multi-profile functionality for families
- Sync your progress across devices (when signed in)
- Analyze usage patterns to improve app features
- Send you updates about new lessons and features (with your consent)
- Ensure the app functions properly and troubleshoot issues

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Most user data, including practice progress and settings, is stored locally on your device.
- **Cloud Storage**: When you sign in with Google, your profile and progress data may be synced to Firebase for backup and cross-device access.
- **Security**: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption for data transmission and storage.
- **Data Retention**: We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services and as required by law.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Firebase**: For authentication, cloud storage, and analytics. Firebase processes data according to their privacy policy.
- **Google Analytics**: To understand user behavior and improve our app. Google Analytics may collect anonymous usage data.

## Children's Privacy

Pianio is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children. We:

- Do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
- Provide age-appropriate content and features
- Allow parents to manage their children's profiles
- Comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) requirements

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- With service providers who perform services on our behalf (e.g., Firebase)
- When required by law or to protect our rights
- With your explicit consent

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Opt out of data collection (note: this may affect app functionality)
- Export your data

## Microphone and Permissions

Pianio may request the following permissions:

- **Microphone**: For acoustic piano pitch detection. This data is processed locally and not stored.
- **Bluetooth**: For connecting to Bluetooth MIDI devices.
- **Storage**: For saving lessons and user data locally.

All permissions are optional and can be revoked in your device settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and within the app.
