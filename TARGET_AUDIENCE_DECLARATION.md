# Target Audience and Content Declaration

## Target Audience

### Primary Target Audience
**Children and Teens (Ages 5-17)**
- Children learning piano for the first time
- Teens interested in music education
- Students supplementing formal piano lessons
- Families looking for educational apps

### Secondary Target Audience
**Adults (Ages 18+)**
- Adult beginners wanting to learn piano
- Hobbyists and music enthusiasts
- Adults returning to piano after a break
- Anyone interested in self-paced music learning

## Content Classification

### App Category
**Education** - Music and Arts

### Content Rating
**Everyone** - Suitable for all audiences

### Content Characteristics

**Educational Content:**
- Music theory lessons
- Piano technique instruction
- Note reading and sight reading
- Ear training exercises
- Rhythm and timing practice

**Interactive Features:**
- Gamified learning with XP and achievements
- Real-time feedback on playing
- Progress tracking and statistics
- Multi-user profiles for families

**Creative Content:**
- Free play mode with various instruments
- Recording and playback features
- Custom song import capability
- DJ remix studio with beats and backing tracks

## Safety and Appropriateness

### Child Safety
- No violent or inappropriate content
- No user-generated content sharing
- No social networking features
- No in-app purchases or gambling
- No advertisements
- Safe and controlled learning environment

### Data Privacy for Children
- Minimal data collection for children
- No location tracking
- No contact with other users
- Parental control through profile management
- COPPA compliant

## Accessibility

### Age Appropriateness
- Content is suitable for ages 5 and up
- Difficulty levels adjust to user skill
- No mature themes or language
- Educational focus throughout

### Skill Level Appropriateness
- Beginner-friendly interface
- Progressive difficulty curve
- Clear instructions and guidance
- No prior musical knowledge required

## Geographic Availability
- Global availability
- Multi-language support (English initially)
- Culturally neutral content
- No region-specific restrictions

## Special Considerations

### Permissions
- Microphone: For pitch detection (optional)
- Bluetooth: For MIDI keyboard connection (optional)
- Storage: For local data saving (required)

### Offline Functionality
- Core lessons available offline
- Progress saved locally
- No internet required for basic use

### Family Sharing
- Multi-profile support
- Each profile has independent progress
- No cross-profile data sharing

## Compliance

This app complies with:
- Google Play Families Policy
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- Google Play Developer Content Policy
- Educational content guidelines

## Content Updates

Regular content updates include:
- New lessons and songs
- Additional skill levels
- Feature improvements
- Bug fixes and performance enhancements

All content updates maintain the same educational and family-friendly standards.
